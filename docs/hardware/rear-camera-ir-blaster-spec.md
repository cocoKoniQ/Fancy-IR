# Rear Camera Plateau IR Blaster Hardware Specification (`Fancy-IR`)

This document maps the exact physical coordinates of rear-mounted infrared (IR) blasters relative to the primary ($1\times$) camera lens across modern Android smartphones. 

All coordinates are described as viewed when **looking directly at the back of the phone held vertically in Portrait orientation**.

## 5. Optical & UX Engineering Implications for `Fancy-IR`

### A. Zero Parallax Beyond $1\text{ Meter}$
* Across all rear-plateau phones, the physical offset ($\Delta d$) between the optical center of the main $1\times$ camera sensor and the $\sim 940\text{ nm}$ IR emitter is between $14\text{ mm}$ and $28\text{ mm}$.
* Smartphone IR emitters use wide-angle dome lenses with a half-intensity beam angle of $\pm 15^\circ$ to $\pm 25^\circ$.
* At a standard room distance of $2\text{ m}$, the IR cone is $\sim 1.1\text{ m}$ wide. A $22\text{ mm}$ offset produces an angular error of $< 0.6^\circ$, meaning **dead-center in the $1\times$ camera reticle is dead-center in the IR beam**.

### B. Close-Range ("Point-Blank") Reticle Offset ($< 30\text{ cm}$)
* If a user holds the phone $< 20\text{ cm}$ from a small IR receiver (e.g., testing a discrete receiver eye up close), the $15\text{--}25\text{ mm}$ horizontal offset becomes noticeable.
* On almost all top-left squircle phones (Xiaomi 14/15/17, POCO F6 Pro/F7, OnePlus 15, Realme GT 7 Pro), the IR blaster sits **$15\text{--}25\text{ mm}$ to the right of the main $1\times$ camera** when viewed from the back (which translates to **$15\text{--}25\text{ mm}$ to the left of the camera lens** from the user's perspective looking at the screen in Portrait mode).

### C. Mandatory Portrait Orientation Lock (Finger Occlusion Prevention)
* On popular models like the **Xiaomi 14 / 15 / 17** and **iQOO 13**, the IR blaster sits in the **Bottom-Right corner** of the camera island.
* If the user rotates the phone into **Landscape mode**, their left index finger naturally rests against the edge of the camera bump and frequently covers the Bottom-Right sensor ring while leaving the main camera lens unobstructed.
* **Design Rule:** Lock `Fancy-IR` to **Portrait orientation** so the user's hand grips the lower half of the phone body, keeping fingers completely clear of the camera plateau.

### D. Hardware Profile Lookup (`android.os.Build.MODEL`)
* We can maintain a lightweight JSON/Kotlin lookup table mapping `Build.MANUFACTURER` / `Build.MODEL` to:
  1. `emitterMount`: `REAR_PLATEAU` vs. `TOP_EDGE` (so if someone installs the app on an older top-mounted IR phone, the UI can adapt or warn them).
  2. `offsetMm`: `(x, y)` offset in millimeters from the primary $1\times$ camera lens for optional onboarding visuals ("Here is where your invisible laser fires from on your device").
